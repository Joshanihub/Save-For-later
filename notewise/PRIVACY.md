# Privacy Policy for Notewise

**Last Updated:** June 4, 2026

Thank you for using Notewise ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it when you use our Chrome Extension.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use Notewise, we collect the following types of information:

*   **Authentication Information:** To provide cross-device syncing, Notewise requires you to create an account. We collect your email address and an encrypted password used exclusively to authenticate your account.
*   **User-Generated Content:** The core functionality of Notewise is note-taking. We collect and store the text, tags, and reading list links you explicitly save within the extension.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for a **single purpose**: to provide and maintain the core functionality of the Notewise extension. Specifically:

*   Your email is used solely to authenticate you and secure your account.
*   Your notes and reading list data are securely stored in our cloud database (powered by Supabase) so they can be synced seamlessly across your devices. 
*   We **do not** use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

## 3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides enterprise-grade security and encryption. 
*   All communication between the extension and the database is encrypted via HTTPS.
*   We do not store passwords in plain text.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy. **We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or user-generated content to any third parties.** 

Your data is never transferred or used for purposes unrelated to the single purpose of providing the Notewise service. We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for any lending purposes.

## 5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You may at any time:
*   Access, edit, or delete any note or collection from within the extension.
*   Request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us (or using the deletion tools within the app, if provided).

## 6. Permissions Justification

To function correctly, Notewise requests the following permissions in your browser:
*   **`storage`**: Used to save your UI preferences (like themes) and cache notes locally so the app works offline.
*   **`alarms` & `notifications`**: Used exclusively to run the Pomodoro focus timer and trigger your daily reminders.
*   **`activeTab` & `scripting`**: Used only when you explicitly choose to save the current webpage to your Reading List, allowing us to grab the page URL and title.
*   **Host Permission (`*.supabase.co`)**: Required to connect the extension to our backend database for secure authentication and data syncing.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. 

## 8. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, or if you would like to request the deletion of your data, please contact the developer directly.
